# Changelog

All notable changes to this project are documented in this file. The format is based on [Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.1.0/).

## [Unreleased]

### Added
- The project: a Forge 1.20.1 mod (`bsp_weapons`) that requires BSP-Core 1.17.1 and GeckoLib 4.7.3 and works with BSP-Region Guard, BSP-RankUp, BSP-InventorySync and Mekanism when they are present. Headless GameTests run with `./gradlew runGameTestServer`.
- The part system's data model. A weapon is a pattern (its class, slots and base numbers), a core tier (I to V), parts (each a kind, a type and a material, with its own roll), the weapon's roll (Normal, Rare, Epic, S) and augment chips; its sheet (damage, armour piercing, range, fire rate, charge time, capacity, recharge, spread, zoom, heat capacity, block damage, ricochets, durability) is worked out on the server from those alone. Patterns, materials, bands, part types, augments and catalysts are data files under `data/bsp_weapons/weapons/`, so a datapack can add or change any of them: 105 materials keyed to `forge:ingots/` and `forge:gems/` tags in five bands, 14 part types, 9 augments, 4 catalysts and the laser pistol pattern. Server config `bsp_weapons-server.toml`: the tier and rarity multipliers, augment slots per tier, the piercing and range caps.
- The part items: fourteen parts (Focused, Scatter, Thermal and Pulse Barrels; Energy, Overcharge and EMP Cells and the Magazine; Standard, Light and Heavy Grips; Iron Sight, Scope and Holo Sight) and the Weapon Core (Tier I to V). A part's icon is its own voxel model, tinted by the material it is made of; the tooltip shows its rarity and material and its own changes to the sheet. A BSP-Weapons creative tab. The part data (materials, bands, part types) is sent to every client on joining and on `/reload`, so icons and tooltips match the server's data; players need the same network protocol (1) as the server.
- The Weapon Assembler: a machine two blocks wide, a block and a half high and one deep (a pod with the work under a glass dome on the left, a console on the right, two arms folded above), placed facing the player. Its screen lays the slots out as the weapon is built: the Engram at the rail's end, the Core at its heart, the Optic above, the Barrel ahead, the Grip and Cell below, a roll slot and the finished weapon at the right, with the weapon's sheet as bars and the roll odds. ASSEMBLE uses the Core and one of each part, keeps the Engram, and spends a catalyst if one is in the roll slot to set the roll; TAKE APART spends a catalyst and returns the Core and parts, each with its own roll, while the weapon's roll is lost.
- Engrams (one per weapon class; the Laser Pistol Engram first), the four catalysts (Lapis, Magnatite, Illyrium, Allthemodium: lapis and one ingredient in a vial, with the odds on the tooltip) and the Laser Pistol as an item that holds its parts and shows its sheet; firing comes later. Materials take BSP-Core's colours: Tetrium violet, Magnatite steel blue, Illyrium turquoise.
- Firing. A Laser Pistol shoots a lance of light on right-click: it travels at about two and a half blocks a tick, can be dodged, hurts the first thing it meets, stops at a block and fades at the weapon's Range. Each shot spends a charge, adds heat and a point of durability; at the heat capacity the weapon locks for a moment and loses extra durability; an empty weapon reloads over its Recharge time. Damage is the weapon's own type: armour piercing lets its share of a hit through armour whole, while Protection, Resistance and BSP-Core's totem Resistance see the whole hit. Players are hurt only where BSP-Region Guard allows PvP for both ends of the shot. Kills are the shooter's. Weapons take Unbreaking, Mending and the curses, and no other enchantment. A HUD shows heat and charge while a BSP weapon is held; the held gun's Cell windows and bore flash red when hot and its Core breathes red while locked. Server config `[firing]`: shot heat, cooling time, lock time, overheat durability, wear per shot, lance speed.
- Energy cells. A weapon whose Cell holds energy (Energy, Overcharge or EMP Cell) stores Forge Energy, Capacity shots' worth at 250 FE a shot, and charges in any FE charger at up to a full store per Recharge time; it never refills on its own, and the tooltip shows its FE. The HUD reads NO CHARGE when it is empty. A Magazine cell holds rounds instead. Config: `energyPerShot`, and whether a weapon leaves the assembler full.
- Rounds. The Energy Round, a stackable capsule that every weapon built with a Magazine loads: an empty magazine weapon reloads by itself over its Recharge time when its holder carries rounds, taking up to Capacity from the inventory, and does nothing without them. The tooltip shows the rounds loaded; the HUD reads NO ROUNDS when none are carried.
