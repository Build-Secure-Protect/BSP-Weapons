THE LASER PISTOL: FIRE, HIT, RELOAD, OVERHEAT

Three families of effect for the first energy weapon, each in the first-person view with a target post eight blocks ahead. Every effect is pixels: cubes, flat squares and short lines, never a smooth glow. FIRE shoots once, RELOAD drops the Cell and brings a full one back, OVERHEAT fires eight shots fast so the gun locks, AUTO keeps shooting on its own. The heat bar and charge count at the foot are the HUD as decided (D47).

OVERHEATED

A bright teal slug three pixels long leaves the muzzle behind a four-pixel cross of flash and crosses to the target in a tenth of a second; where it lands, eight pixel sparks burst outward and fall. Reload drops the Cell out and slides a fresh one up. Overheating turns the barrel's vents red pixel by pixel and lets grey puffs rise from them; the gun locks until they clear. Reads as a weapon that shoots something; the slug is visible to everyone and can be dodged at range.

BBEAM

OVERHEATED

A one-pixel teal line from muzzle to target for four frames, with a fainter two-pixel halo and a flat square flare at the muzzle that shrinks away; the hit leaves a glowing square scorch on the target for a second and throws six sparks. Overheating tints the beam and the halo toward red and the lock shows as a red pulse in the bore. Reads as a true laser: no travel, nothing to dodge, the hit lands where the crosshair is.

A thick short lance of teal pixels crosses in a sixth of a second with a ring of eight pixels expanding behind it from the muzzle; where it lands a second ring opens flat on the surface and fades. Overheating makes the Cell's windows flash red and the whole gun shiver; the lock is a slow red breathing of the Core. Reads as plasma rather than light: slower, heavier, showier, and the one that would suit the grenades and the rifle's burst too.

WHAT TO PICK

  1. The effect family for energy weapons (A, B or C), or a mix such as "B's beam with A's sparks".
  2. Travel. As drawn, A and C travel (a slug or lance you can see cross the gap, and dodge at long range) and B is instant. For the pistol: instant (hitscan) is the default; or travelling at A's speed.
  3. Overheat tell. As drawn, each family has its own; or one shared tell for every energy weapon (the barrel vents turning red).
