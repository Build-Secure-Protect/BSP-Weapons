THE ASSEMBLER, REFINED

The Pod as picked, grown to two blocks wide, one and a half high and one deep (32 by 24 by 16 voxels), with two mechanical arms over the work, a control console with a sloped display, button banks, dials and a lever, and a holographic display above the console that shows the weapon in the slots. The pistol under the dome pulls apart and closes while the arms work; a blocky player stands beside it for scale. Drag to turn.

THE MACHINE

32 x 24 x 16 voxels, two block spaces

Left half, the pod: a vented gunmetal base with a gold seam, a teal glass shell over the front and top with the weapon inside on a lit plate, and a dark housing at the back the arms rise from. Right half, the console: a stepped desk with a sloped teal display drawn as a pixel grid, two banks of gold and red buttons, three dials, a lever, and a status lamp. Above: two arms on gold shoulder rings, each with an upper arm, an elbow, a forearm and a teal-tipped tool, folded when idle and reaching over the work when assembling; and the hologram, a tilted teal grid panel with the weapon floating in front of it as a translucent copy.

HOW IT IS BUILT

  • Two block spaces. The left space holds the block entity, the screen and the inventory; the right space is a companion block that opens the same screen. Breaking either drops the machine as one item. It faces the player who placed it, pod on the left.
  • Height. The base is 8 voxels, the dome and console reach 20, the arms' shoulders and the hologram's top reach 24: a block and a half.
  • Motion. Idle: the lamp breathes teal, the hologram turns slowly, the arms rest folded. Assembling (a few seconds after ASSEMBLE): the arms unfold and dip to the work twice, the parts fly in, the lamp turns gold, then everything settles. Taking apart plays it backwards.
  • Light. The display, hologram, dome floor, arm tips and lamp are full bright; the machine gives off a little light (level 7) like a lit furnace.
  • Sound. A soft servo on each arm move and a chime when the weapon is done; silent when idle.

WHAT THE OWNER ASKED FOR

  • "mechanical arms to help assemble the guns": two, over the work, animated.
  • "more buttons, a control display": two button banks, dials, a lever, a sloped grid display and the hologram.
  • "2 blocks wide, 1.5 blocks high including the arms, holographic display etc, and 1 deep": 32 x 24 x 16.
