THE WEAPON ENHANCER, REFINED

The picks of 18 November: the Spire for the block (D84), the Board for the screen (D85), the Shard Key for the chip (D86), the chips' share in violet with a red cost mark (D87). The Spire is drawn at full detail, one block wide and two high, with a player beside it for scale and its three operations animated: FIT seats a chip, UPGRADE swaps the Core, REPAIR melts an ingot in. The Board gains a chosen socket and a FULL state. The Shard Key is grown to 8 by 14 by 4 voxels, shown for all seven augments, with its tooltip at every rarity. Drag any stage to turn it.

THE MACHINE: SPIRE

16 x 32 x 16 voxels, one block wide, two high

The base: a vented gunmetal block with a gold seam, the charge window on its front, the core hatch with a violet lamp on its left and the repair hopper with an ingot on its right. The socket: a gold collar on the base the weapon stands in, barrel up, over a teal seat lamp. The spine: rises at the back with the energy gauge in sixteen gold segments, gold stripes and vents up its sides. The arm and clamp: reach over the weapon from the top of the spine and hold the muzzle. The ring: gold with four teal eyes, parked under the arm when idle, running down and up the weapon while working. The drum: on top, four bays holding Shard Keys in their augment colours, one bay empty, turning while working.

HOW IT IS BUILT

  • Two block spaces, one above the other. The lower holds the block entity, the inventory and the screen; the upper is a companion block that opens the same screen, as the assembler's second half does. Breaking either drops the machine as one item. It faces the player who placed it.
  • Heights. Base 0 to 6, socket 7 to 8, the weapon stands 9 to 21, the ring travels 9 to 21, clamp 21 to 25, arm 26 to 27, drum 28 to 31. The drum, the clamp and the top of the spine sit in the upper space.
  • Idle. The gauge shows the charge, the ring waits under the arm, the drum is still, the seat lamp breathes teal.
  • Working, about three seconds. The ring runs the length of the weapon, the drum turns, and the item flies into the weapon's heart: a Shard Key from the drum on FIT, the new Core from the left hatch on UPGRADE while the old one leaves for the hatch, an ingot from the right hopper on REPAIR that bursts into gold sparks. The seat lamp flashes when the item lands. The charge (D75) is drawn when the operation starts.
  • The sides are the look of the slots. The hatch is the CORE slot, the hopper the REPAIR slot, the drum the chip sockets; items go in through the screen, not by hand.
  • Light and sound. Light level 7 like a lit furnace; a hum while working, a chime when done, a short buzz when the machine refuses (a Breacher or Ricochet chip, a full weapon, no charge).

WHAT THE OWNER PICKED

  • "1. B the Spire": the standing weapon, the travelling ring, the drum on top, the gauge up the spine, grown from the set 7 sketch.
  • "2. B Board": the weapon large on its rail with the sockets wired to it.
  • "3. C Shard Key": the crystal in a clasp, now with a gold band, a keyway and two gold teeth.

THE SCREEN: BOARD

D85, 256 x 200 at 2x
at GUI scale 1 in a 320 x 240 window

As picked, with the rules drawn in. The sockets show Shard Keys with their durability pips above; the chosen socket has a teal frame and REMOVE takes its chip. FIT takes the chip from the CHIP IN slot into the first free socket and is greyed when every socket is full. CORE sits under the rail's heart and lights UPGRADE when a higher Core is in it; REPAIR sits under the grip and lights when an ingot of a fitted material is in it. The gauge on the right is the charge. The bars show the parts' share in teal, the Core and roll's in gold, the chips' in violet, and a red mark where a chip costs a stat (D87). The weapon stands still, side on, so the traces always reach its frame.

WHAT THE STATES SHOW

  • LOADED. A Laser Pistol with a Core III, roll S, three sockets, two chips fitted (Piercing Rare 9/10, Overcharge Normal 7/10), a Core IV waiting in CORE and an ingot in REPAIR. Socket 1 chosen.
  • FULL. A third chip (Coolant Epic 10/10) fitted; FIT greyed; the Coolant lifts HEAT CAP back above the Overcharge's cost.
  • NO POWER. The gauge empty, every key greyed, the status in red.
  • EMPTY. No weapon: the rail bare, the sockets faint, the bars empty.

WHAT A LINE MEANS

  • Chip rows. Name in the augment's colour, then durability pips, the fraction and the rarity in violet (D81).
  • The status. Two lines: what the lit key will cost, and what the chosen socket holds or why a key is grey.
  • Core line. "Core III S 3 slots" under the weapon's name; after UPGRADE it reads "Core IV S 3 slots" (tier IV gives 2 slots, the S roll a third; tier V gives 4).

THE CHIP: SHARD KEY

D86, 8 x 14 x 4 voxels
AUGMENT

One item, tinted. A gunmetal clasp with a dark base, two gold teeth below it that are the contacts, a gold band where the crystal meets the clasp, a keyway cut into the front and a gold index pin on the right. The crystal is the augment's colour, four tiers tapering to a single voxel tip, its corner edges glowing. Nothing on the model changes with rarity (D79); the tooltip carries it.

ICONS: 16 PX FOR EACH AUGMENT, 32 PX FOR THE CHOSEN ONE

TOOLTIP

RARITY

Reads: the chip's name; the rarity line in violet as a part's (D81); each effect scaled by the rarity multiplier, teal where it adds and red where it costs; durability with pips in the augment's colour (D83); what it fits. Breacher and Ricochet keep their JSON but no icon or tooltip is shown here: they are not in the creative tab (D73).
