WEAPON PARTS: THREE WAYS TO BUILD

How a BSP-Weapons gun comes together from its parts. The same laser-pistol pattern in each concept: a Core sets the tier, a Barrel, Cell, Grip and Optic are made of the pack's metals, and the frame is how they join. Each model turns, pulls apart and closes again; drag to turn it yourself. The sheet under each shows the same numbers in that concept's own style.

ASPINE RAIL

parts clamp onto one rail

SHEET: BARS

Pistol base × Core II → + parts → × Rare → + chips

One rail, five clamps. The frame is a grooved spine with notches; every part sits in a gold clamp ring at a fixed point: Core in a cradle on top, Barrel forward, Cell under, Grip behind, Optic on the rear mount. Swapping a part slides it off its clamp. Honest and readable at a glance: you can tell a Tier V core from across a room because it rides on top. Silhouette: industrial, Halo and Mass Effect.

BSHELL CHASSIS

parts seat inside a body

SHEET: RADAR

Pistol base × Core II → + parts → × Rare → + chips

A chassis with bays. The frame is a chamfered body: the Barrel screws into a collar, the Cell drops into a bay underneath, the Grip plugs into a socket, the Optic slides along a dovetail, and the Core sits behind a side window under a hinged plate. Parts show as inlays, so the gun keeps one clean shape whatever is fitted. Silhouette: sleek, Destiny and Star Trek.

CHARDLIGHT CLUSTER

parts held by energy struts

SHEET: RINGS

Pistol base × Core II → + parts → × Rare → + chips

No frame at all. The pattern is a field: the Core floats in a gimbal and holds the Barrel, Cell, Grip and Optic on glowing struts. Parts hover a finger's width apart and the struts flicker; pull a part and its strut stretches, then snaps. The most alien of the three, and the one where the tier glow matters most, because the core is the centre of everything. Silhouette: exotic, Destiny and Cyberpunk.

WHAT TO PICK

  1. The build style (A, B or C), or a mix such as "B's body with A's clamps showing".
  2. The sheet style (bars, radar or rings) for the assembler screen and the tooltip.
  3. Whether the material tint should colour the whole part or only its trim, and whether the Core's tier glow should show through the frame as drawn here.
