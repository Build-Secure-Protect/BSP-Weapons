PATTERNS AND CATALYSTS

The two items the assembler needs besides parts: the weapon pattern that names a class and its slots (D17), and the catalyst that sets the roll's odds (D21, D30). Three looks for each, every one a small voxel model so its icon is the model (D48), shown turning and as 16 and 32 pixel icons in a hotbar slot. Patterns are found in loot as well as made, so they should read as something worth finding.

PATTERN PLATES

the laser pistol's; other classes change the glyph
A Blueprint Card. A thin dark plate with the weapon drawn on it in raised teal lines, a gold tab at one corner and a gold class mark. Reads as a schematic.
B Data Chip. A gunmetal cartridge with gold contacts along one end, a notch, and a teal window showing the class glyph. Reads as something you plug into the console.
C Etched Plate. A bright metal plate with the weapon's silhouette cut into it to the dark layer beneath and a gold rivet at each corner. Reads as a stamped template, Tinkers'-adjacent.

AS ICONS

In loot a pattern should be spotted in a chest at a glance; the gold on each is there for that.

CATALYSTS

lapis plus one ingredient; the colour is the ingredient's
INGREDIENT
A Crystal. A lapis crystal cluster, a tall spire and two shards, with the ingredient glowing at its heart. Reads as a gem; sits well in a slot.
B Vial. A small glass vial with a gold cap, lapis liquid inside and ingredient-coloured motes rising through it. Reads as a reagent; the liquid level could show as it is used.
C Bound Shards. A lapis shard and an ingredient shard crossed and bound with a gold band. Reads as a charm; the two colours tell the ladder step apart at once.

AS ICONS, THE WHOLE LADDER

Redstone, Magnatite, Illyrium, Allthemodium (D30), each at 16 pixels for the picked look. The odds tooltip under each item reads, for example, "Lapis Catalyst · 70 / 25 / 5 / 0".

WHAT TO PICK

  1. The pattern plate (A, B or C), or a mix such as "C's plate with A's gold tab".
  2. The catalyst (A, B or C).
  3. Naming. Default: "Laser Pistol Pattern" and "Lapis Catalyst", "Magnatite Catalyst", "Illyrium Catalyst", "Allthemodium Catalyst". Or: names of your own.
