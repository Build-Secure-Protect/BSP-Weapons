PART ICONS: THREE STYLES

The item icons of the weapon parts, 16 by 16 pixels as every Minecraft item is. The same fifteen parts in each style (the laser pistol's four slots in every type, and the Core), shown in a hotbar at the game's own scale and enlarged. The material tints the part, gold fittings stay gold, glows stay teal, the Core glows its tier. Change the material and tier above; every style follows.

ASIDE PROFILE

the part itself, drawn flat
Focused BarrelRare · TetriumRange +28% Spread -30%Damage +14% Durability +60

Like Tinkers' parts. Each part is its own silhouette from the side, in the material's shades with a dark outline, the way a vanilla item is drawn. Barrels are long and low, cells upright, grips angled, optics squat. Quick to read in a chest: the shape says the kind, the colour says the material, the glow says energy.

BFRAMED CHIP

the part on a dark card
Focused BarrelRare · TetriumRange +28% Spread -30%Damage +14% Durability +60

A manufactured component. The same silhouette sits on a dark plate with a one-pixel rim and a coloured tab at the foot that names the kind: teal for barrels, gold for cells, violet for grips, blue for optics, white for the core. Reads as "made in the parts machine" and groups well in a sorted inventory; the part is a little smaller inside its frame.

CFROM THE MODEL

the voxel part, rendered small
Focused BarrelRare · TetriumRange +28% Spread -30%Damage +14% Durability +60

Rendered from the real parts. The pistol's own voxel parts from the refined pick, drawn from a three-quarter angle into 16 pixels, as block items are. Icons always match the weapon they build, and a generator makes them from the model files with no hand pixelling; the cost is that thin parts turn to a few pixels at 16 and read best at 32. Shown here for the five pistol parts; the other types follow once their models exist.

WHAT TO PICK

  1. The icon style (A, B or C), or a mix such as "A's shapes on B's card" or "C, but at 32 pixels".
  2. Whether the kind should show on the icon at all (B's tab) or only in the name and tooltip.
  3. The tooltip: the part's name, its rarity and material on one violet line, and its own changes to the sheet underneath, as drawn.
