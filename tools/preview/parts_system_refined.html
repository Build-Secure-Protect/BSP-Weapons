SPINE RAIL PISTOL, IN PIXELS

The refined pick from set 1: build A drawn as a voxel model with pixel shading in the feel of vanilla Minecraft. One voxel is one sixteenth of a block; the pistol is 36 voxels long and 20 tall with the grip, drawn large on the turntable and at about two thirds of a block in the hand (a rifle will be held at about one block). The same model is shown three ways. Drag any view to turn it. Set RARITY to EPIC or S to see the edge glow and the runes.

THE PISTOL

turntable, pulls apart

Five parts on one rail. The frame is a 4 by 4 rail with a cut groove and notches every four voxels, gold side rails and a rear block. The Core sits in a gold cradle on top and glows its tier colour. The Barrel is 4 by 4 with two vent fins a side, a gold clamp ring where it meets the rail and a gold muzzle ring round a 2 by 2 glowing bore. The Cell hangs under the middle with a teal charge window on each side and a gold latch. The Grip steps back one voxel every two rows, with dark finger grooves and a gold trigger. The Optic sits on a dark rear mount with a teal lens.

HEAT

What firing looks like. A shot kicks the pistol back and up for a tenth of a second, the bore flashes, a teal beam leaves the muzzle, and the heat bar fills; keep firing and it runs red and the gun locks for a moment. RELOAD drops the Cell out of the rail, the charge count resets and the Cell slides back in. These are the animations GeckoLib will play: fire, reload, overheat; an inspect turn can come later.

ON THE PLAYER

third person, aiming

Scale against a player. A blocky player two blocks tall holds the pistol in the right hand at a little over one block long, about the size of a vanilla crossbow. The tier glow and the muzzle bore read from this distance, which is what matters in a fight. The player sways as vanilla idles do.

AS DECIDED (R1 TO R8)

  1. R1 Scale. 36 by 20 by 6 voxels for a pistol; a rifle about 56 long. Held 40 % smaller than the first draft: about two thirds of a block for the pistol, about one block for a rifle.
  2. R2 Pixel shading. Each part is its material colour in three shades scattered as pixels, plus vanilla face lighting.
  3. R3 What glows. The Core (tier colour), the bore, the Cell windows and the Optic lens glow full bright.
  4. R4 Gold fittings. Clamp rings, side rails, cradle, latch and trigger are gold whatever the material.
  5. R5 Rarity. Epic and S weapons carry a faint edge glow in the rarity colour (violet, gold) and glowing embossed runes on the barrel sides, the rear block and the Cell, pulsing slowly. Normal and Rare show nothing on the model; the tooltip border shows every rarity. To confirm: the rune look as drawn here.
  6. R6 Animations for the laser pistol. Fire (kick back and up, bore flash, beam), reload (Cell drops and returns, 1.2 s), overheat (red bore pulse, lock 1.5 s), idle sway.
  7. R7 First-person position. Lower right, grip pointing down and back, barrel level with the crosshair.
  8. R8 Heat and charge HUD. A heat bar and a charge count at the bottom corners, only while a BSP weapon is held, clear of the other BSP overlays.
