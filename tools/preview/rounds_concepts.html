ROUNDS FOR THE MAGAZINE

A weapon built with a Magazine cell (D10) loads rounds instead of charging: a stackable item made at the parts machine, spent one per shot, reloaded from the inventory. Three looks, each a small voxel model so its icon is the model, turning and as 16 and 32 pixel icons. The same item serves every weapon class with a magazine; a later task can tier it.

THE ROUND

one item, stacks to 64
A Capsule. A short gunmetal cartridge with a teal cap at the business end and a gold band: a sci-fi bullet with no brass. Reads instantly as ammunition.
B Clip. Four capsules side by side in a dark clip with a gold spine: the item is a strip, and one shot takes one capsule off it in the icon's count. Reads as something you slot in.
C Charge Shard. A teal crystal shard with a gunmetal sleeve: energy made solid, the ammunition of an energy weapon. Reads as part of the same world as the Core and the catalysts.

AS ICONS

Each at 16 and at 32 pixels. In a hotbar the item shows its stack count as any ammunition does.

WHAT TO PICK

  1. The round (A, B or C).
  2. Name. Default: "Energy Round". Or a name of your own.
  3. Reloading. Default: an empty magazine weapon reloads by itself from the player's inventory over its Recharge time, taking Capacity rounds. Or: a reload key the player presses.
