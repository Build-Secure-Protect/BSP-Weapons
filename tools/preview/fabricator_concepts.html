THE WEAPON FABRICATOR AND THE BLANK ENGRAM

The first machine of the brief, decided as D97 to D107: a bench two blocks wide, one high and one deep on Forge Energy, that makes parts from any material by tag with an optional catalyst for the part's roll, chips, engrams from a blank, Cores, catalysts and rounds, each job a set charge and about four seconds of work. Three picks: the machine, the screen, the Blank Engram. Every model is pixels (D37). The machines are shown turning with a job running (WORKING: a Tetrium Focused Barrel being made, the finished part appearing on the tray); the screens are drawn at the game's pixel size, 256 by 232 with the player's inventory where it goes, and inside a 320 by 240 window; the blank is shown turning beside the Data Chip it becomes, and as 16 and 32 pixel icons.

THE MACHINE set 9: 32 x 16 x 16 voxels, two block spaces

ALATHE

the work turns, the tool walks

A machinist's bench. The energy tower on the left with its gold gauge, an ingot hopper on the front, a headstock and gold chuck that spin the workpiece, a tool carriage that walks along the bed throwing sparks, a tailstock, the finished part dropping onto the output tray on the right, and a console with a teal readout and buttons at the back right.

BPRESS AND BELT

a press on the left, moulds ride a belt

A foundry. A C-frame press on the left whose gold head drops into a mould on the anvil with a teal flash, a belt on the right carrying mould tiles toward an output chute, a tall gauge on the back right, red and gold buttons along the front, the ingot hopper beside the press.

CGANTRY PRINTER

the part grows layer by layer

A fabricator proper. A frame over a dark bed with a teal grid, a bridge that runs along the bed and a head that runs along the bridge, laying the part down in three layers with a teal nozzle and sparks, a material spool in the left tower with the gauge beside it, a console with a readout and buttons at the right front, the finished part lifted off to the tray.

THE BLANK ENGRAM set 11: the Data Chip it becomes is on the left

FOUR ITEMS

drag to turn; the Data Chip is D61 as built

A Unwritten Chip: the same cartridge with steel contacts, no gold band and a dark empty window: the blank is plainly the engram before it is written. B Slate: a flat dark board with a light grid etched in it, a gold tab at one corner, a glass pane over it: a blank to be filled, the machine writes the glyph into the grid. C Spool: a small reel of teal tape on a gunmetal hub: raw data, wound; the engram is cut from it.

ICONS: 16 PX AND 32 PX

HOW IT IS USED

Crafting table (D102): four iron ingots, a redstone and a glass pane make one Blank Engram. Fabricator: a Blank Engram in the BLANK slot and four of the class's signature material in MATERIAL make that class's Engram (Laser Pistol: Tetrium); the engram list on the screen shows every pattern the server knows. The blank stacks to 16.

WHAT TO PICK

  1. The machine: A Lathe, B Press and Belt, or C Gantry Printer, or a mix.
  2. The screen: A Catalogue, B Workbench, or C Ledger. The part's own changes as bars (C) can join A or B.
  3. The Blank Engram: A Unwritten Chip, B Slate, or C Spool.
  4. The working look: the part appearing on the tray at the end (as drawn), or in the OUT slot only with the machine's motion as the whole tell.
