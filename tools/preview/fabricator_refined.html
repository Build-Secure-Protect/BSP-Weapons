THE WEAPON FABRICATOR, REFINED

The picks of 19 November: the Gantry Printer for the block (D108), the Catalogue for the screen (D109), the Slate for the Blank Engram (D110), the product on the tray at the end of a job (D111). The printer is drawn at full detail, two blocks wide, with a player beside it for scale and three jobs animated: PART grows a Tetrium barrel in three layers, CHIP grows a Shard Key, ENGRAM writes a glyph into a Slate. The Catalogue gains an ENGRAM state showing the BLANK slot at work, a roll odds line and a scroll bar. The Slate is grown to 10 by 2 by 10 voxels, with its tooltip. Drag any stage to turn it.

THE MACHINE: GANTRY PRINTER

32 x 16 x 16 voxels, two block spaces

The bed: dark with a teal grid of lit points, where the product grows. The frame: four gunmetal posts with gold rails along the sides; a bridge runs along the rails and the head runs along the bridge, its teal nozzle just over the work. The left tower: the material hopper on top with an ingot in it, the gold energy gauge on the front, the spool of drawn material on the side, teal tape wound on a gunmetal hub. The right front: a stepped console with a teal readout, gold and red buttons and a lever. The back right: the tray with a gold rim, where the finished product lands.

HOW IT IS BUILT

  • Two block spaces side by side as the assembler: the left space (the tower) holds the block entity, the inventory, the charge and the whole model; the right space is a companion block that opens the same screen; breaking either drops the machine as one item. It faces the player who placed it, tower on their left.
  • Heights. Base 0 to 5, bed at 6, the product up to 9, posts and rails to 15, the tower 6 to 15, the console 6 to 11.
  • Idle. The bridge parked at the back, the head at its end, the bed bare, the gauge lit to the charge, the readout breathing teal.
  • Working, about four seconds. The bridge runs the bed and the head runs the bridge in a raster, sparks at the nozzle, the product growing in layers; the lamp in the console turns gold. At the end the product lifts from the bed to the tray and lies there for a moment while it also appears in OUT.
  • Each product has its own little build (R26): a part grows in three layers; a chip grows clasp, crystal, tip; an engram is the Slate laid on the bed and the glyph written into its grid pixel by pixel; a Core grows as a cube; catalysts and rounds as a small stack.
  • Automation (D105): cables and pipes connect to either space; items insert from any side into the slot that takes them, the product extracts from the bottom; the chosen job is kept.
  • Light and sound. Light level 7; a hum and the head's ticking while working, a chime when the product lands, a short buzz on a refusal.

THE SCREEN: CATALOGUE

D109, 256 x 232 at 2x, the inventory at row 150
at GUI scale 1 in a 320 x 240 window

As picked, with the rules drawn in. The tabs PARTS, CHIPS, ENGRAM and MORE each fill the list; the list scrolls with a thin bar and a click chooses a row, kept by the machine. On the right the job: its name, what it needs as ghost items with counts (the "any ingot" ghost cycles through the tag's items), ROLL marked optional, the four slots, the progress with its percentage, MAKE with the charge and time, a roll odds line in violet when a catalyst sits in ROLL, and the status line with the cost or the refusal. The gauge is the charge.

WHAT THE STATES SHOW

  • READY. Focused Barrel chosen, five Tetrium ingots in MATERIAL, two Lapis Catalysts in ROLL, the odds line, MAKE lit, "MAKE: 800 FE, 3 Tetrium Ingot, 1 Lapis Catalyst, 4 s."
  • WORKING. The progress at 60 %, MAKE grey, "Making a Focused Barrel: 2 s left."
  • ENGRAM. The ENGRAM tab, Laser Pistol Engram chosen, a Slate in BLANK and four Tetrium in MATERIAL, no roll (engrams have none), the Data Chip ghost in OUT.
  • IDLE. Nothing chosen: the right side faint, "Choose what to make."
  • NO POWER. The gauge empty, MAKE grey, the status in red.

WHAT A LINE MEANS

  • Needs. A ghost item with "×3" is three of that item; "any ingot" means any material allowed for that part kind (gems for optics too); "optional" on ROLL means the job runs without a catalyst and rolls Normal.
  • Odds. "roll: Normal 70 · Rare 25 · Epic 5 · S 0" is the catalyst in ROLL, as the assembler shows it.
  • The status. The cost of MAKE, or why it is grey: no charge, missing items, OUT full, a material the kind refuses.

THE BLANK ENGRAM: SLATE

D110, 10 x 2 x 10 voxels

One item. A dark board in a gunmetal rim, a light grid etched into it (four by four cells), a gold tab at one corner the machine grips it by, a gold index pin at the opposite corner, and a pane of glass set into the rim over the grid. The Fabricator writes the class's glyph into the grid; what comes out is the class's Data Chip (D61). Beside it: the Slate as the machine leaves it mid-write, the glyph half drawn in teal, which is only ever seen on the bed.

ICONS: 16 PX AND 32 PX

TOOLTIP

Crafting table (D102): four iron ingots, a redstone dust and a glass pane, shapeless, make one Blank Engram; it stacks to 16. Fabricator: a Blank Engram in BLANK and four of the class's signature material in MATERIAL make that class's Engram.
